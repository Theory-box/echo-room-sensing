Hold the phone flat at chest height, bottom edge (speaker and main mic) pointing away from you, arm a little out. Turn slowly on the spot. Echoes are painted in the direction the phone points; your body blocks most sound from behind. Direction is rough (tens of degrees); distance is precise. You'll hear faint ticks.

Map view: stay on one spot and turn slowly, a full circle or two. Every echo is kept; places that keep echoing from different headings get brighter, and places the sonar sees through fade out, so ghosts clear up as you turn. Smooth walls show mostly where they are nearest to you. Clear starts a new map.
