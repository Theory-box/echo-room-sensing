Hold the phone flat at chest height, bottom edge (speaker and main mic) pointing away from you, arm a little out. Turn slowly on the spot. Echoes are painted in the direction the phone points; your body blocks most sound from behind. Direction is rough (tens of degrees); distance is precise. You'll hear faint ticks.

Map view: stay on one spot and turn slowly, a full circle or two. Every echo is kept; places that keep echoing from different headings get brighter, and places the sonar sees through fade out, so ghosts clear up as you turn. Smooth walls show mostly where they are nearest to you. Clear starts a new map. Walls: Straighten draws a straight line (yellow) where an echo kept the same distance while you turned past it: that's a flat wall facing you squarely at the middle of the turn. Lines appear after you turn past a wall; the line's length is a guess, its distance and direction are measured. A corner can also make a line. Room box (test) fits the simplest room that explains all the echoes so far: four walls at right angles. Solid yellow = many echoes back that wall; red dashed = few (a guess, or no wall there). Needs ~15 s of turning. Once you have turned all the way round, the box locks and the app tracks where you are in it: you stay at the centre and the room moves around you (walk toward a wall and it comes toward the dot). Walls firm up as more echoes back them. Clear starts over. Ghost echoes: facing one way, push the phone in and out a few times (5+ cm). Real things in front move with the phone; reflection ghosts and the ceiling stay put and are removed from the map (always on in Map view). Dots: amber = you are near square-on to a surface -- sweep slowly back and forth across it, or push in and out. Green = a good reading of that surface was collected; work your way round until the walls have green dots. More spots: once the room is locked, tap Pause to move (mapping pauses), walk anywhere in the room (stay about an arm's length from the walls), tap Resume here, then turn all the way round once. The app finds where you are from the walls and adds that sweep to the map. Nothing you have mapped is lost.
