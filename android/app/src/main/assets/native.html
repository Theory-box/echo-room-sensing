Echo Room

Room sweep
  1. Phone flat, screen up, raised a little (e.g. on a small container) so both ends hang free.
  2. Tap Start sweep. After each recording the phone says "Turn slowly": rotate it in place (don't slide it) until it says "Stop".
  3. Then duck down out of the way and stay quiet until the next "Turn slowly".
  4. With 2 spots: when asked, move the phone about a foot to the side and tap Continue.
Mic test (all recording modes, about 1 min)
Scratch test (which channel is which mic, ~30 s)

Hold the phone. It names a spot (top edge, bottom edge, camera bump, middle of the back); scratch it lightly with a fingernail until it says "Stop".
