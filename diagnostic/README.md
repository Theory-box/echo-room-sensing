# Echo Diagnostic (test page)

**This is an experimental test page, not a product or a download.**

It checks whether a phone's speakers, microphones and motion sensors work well
enough for acoustic room sensing (echo-based room mapping). It plays a short
sweep, chirps and near-ultrasonic ticks, records the echoes, and prints a
report to copy.

Everything runs locally in the browser. Nothing is uploaded or stored.
