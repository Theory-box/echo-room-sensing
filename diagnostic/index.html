Echo Diagnostic

Before you start
  1. Media volume about 80% (not 100%). No headphones or Bluetooth audio.
  2. Quiet room. Phone flat on a table, screen up, ideally on a folded towel. Nothing covering the top or bottom edges.
  3. Tap Run, allow the microphone, then step back and stay quiet for about 15 seconds. You'll hear a whoosh, some chirps, then faint high ticks.
One question: during the last part, did you hear faint high-pitched ticks?
